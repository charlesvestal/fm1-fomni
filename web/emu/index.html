Speak the screen

Who reads the screen

FoMni-1 tells a screen reader what its screen shows, the way the FM-1 tells the FM-1 Screen Reader: pages, knobs as they turn, chords. Your screen reader reads it while this page is in front; this page's voice (eSpeak NG, running in the page) reads it without one.

Buttons

  • White keys strum, black keys pick the chord.
  • ENV held: minor chord. LFO held: 7th. Both: m7.
  • ARP chord hold: the chord keeps playing after you let go.
  • PLAY rhythm on / off. REC sync start: the rhythm waits for your first chord.
  • OCT− / OCT+ move the strings an octave.
  • Pages: HOME play, SEQ rhythm, FX sound, SEL chords (press a black key, then turn KNOB 1 and 2 to change it), GLO setup. EDIT steps through them.
  • SELECT tempo, ALGORITHM rhythm, PRESETS transpose, KNOB 1–4 the values on the screen. SAVE saves.

Mouse and touch

  • Drag a knob up or down, or use the scroll wheel over it.
  • Click or touch buttons and keys. Drag across the white keys to strum. Several fingers at once work on a touch screen.
  • Combinations with a mouse: Shift-click (or right-click) a button to hold it down, click again to let go.

Keyboard

  • Tab moves through the panel: the knobs, the buttons, the chord keys, the strum keys. Each says its name, and a lit button says so.
  • On a knob: ↑ ↓ (or ← →) turn it, Page Up Page Down faster.
  • On a button or key: Space or Enter holds it down while you hold the key. Shift+Enter keeps a button down until you press it again.
  • Shortcuts, while the panel has focus (turn them off above): strum keys (white) 1–8: Q–I, 9–16: A–K. Chord keys (black) 1–11: 1–0, -.
  • Hold Shift (ENV) for a minor chord, / (LFO) for a 7th, both for an m7.
  • P PLAY, O REC (sync start), Esc HOME, Z / X OCT− / OCT+, ↑ ↓ on a button or key turn SELECT.

Saving

SAVE keeps your chords and sound in this browser, as the FM-1 keeps them in its memory. Factory reset forgets them. It is the firmware, not a recording: what you hear is FoMni-1 running, at the FM-1's 44.1 kHz. The idea of running FM-1 firmware in the browser comes from Groove OS's emulator.
